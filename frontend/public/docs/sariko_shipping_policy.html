Shipping & Delivery Policy

Version 2.0 Effective Date October 16, 2026 Operator Zen Tech Asia Co., Ltd.
This Shipping & Delivery Policy applies to all orders placed through the SariKo platform at www.sariko.store and app.sariko.store. It is issued by Zen Tech Asia Co., Ltd. (MST 0316852554), the operator of SariKo, in compliance with the Law on E-Commerce No. 122/2025/QH15 and Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP.
Section 01

Delivery Coverage Area

SariKo currently provides delivery services within Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC), Vietnam only. Delivery is available across all districts of Ho Chi Minh City, subject to delivery provider availability at the time of order.

SariKo does not currently offer delivery outside of Ho Chi Minh City. Buyers with addresses in Hanoi, Da Nang, or other cities outside HCMC are not able to complete purchases requiring delivery at this time. Buyers may arrange self-collection directly with a seller where both parties agree, but this is outside SariKo's platform delivery service.

Note: Delivery availability to specific addresses within HCMC depends on Lalamove driver availability in the seller's area. If no driver can be booked, the order is cancelled and refunded in full (see Section 06).
Section 02

Delivery Partner

Delivery Partner
Lalamove

On-demand motorcycle delivery across Ho Chi Minh City. All SariKo deliveries are carried out by Lalamove.

SariKo books the Lalamove trip automatically when the seller marks the order as ready. Lalamove provides the delivery status, driver details and a live tracking link, which SariKo shows to both the buyer and the seller in the app.

SariKo is a marketplace intermediary and is not itself a delivery company. Lalamove carries out each delivery under its own terms of service, and SariKo coordinates with Lalamove to resolve delivery problems.

Section 03

Estimated Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates based on distance between seller and buyer within Ho Chi Minh City, and are subject to traffic conditions, driver availability, and order preparation time.

Distance / Scenario Estimated Delivery Time Notes
Same district (seller and buyer) 30 – 60 minutes From order confirmation by seller
Adjacent districts (short cross-district) 45 – 90 minutes Includes pickup + transit time
Cross-district (mid-range) 60 – 120 minutes e.g. Binh Thanh to District 7
Outer districts (Thu Duc, Binh Chanh, Nha Be) 90 – 150 minutes Subject to driver availability
Peak hours: Orders placed during peak lunch (11:00–13:00) and dinner (17:00–20:00) periods may experience longer wait times due to higher demand for delivery drivers across HCMC.
Section 04

Delivery Fees

The delivery fee is quoted by Lalamove for the distance between the seller's address and the buyer's delivery address when the order is placed, and is shown at checkout before payment. SariKo does not mark up delivery fees — the fee is passed to Lalamove in full.

SariKo charges zero platform fee on delivery. The delivery fee and the goods total are the only amounts the buyer pays.

The delivery fee shown at checkout is the only delivery charge the buyer pays; the buyer is never asked to pay more after placing the order. The table above is a guide only.

When an order is cancelled and refunded in full, the refund includes the delivery fee (see Section 06).

Section 05

How Delivery Works — Step by Step

  1. Buyer places and pays for the orderBuyer checks the dishes, delivery address, delivery fee and total, then pays via VNPAY. The order is sent to the seller only after VNPAY confirms the payment. For pre-order dishes, the buyer chooses the delivery date and time.
  2. Seller accepts the orderThe seller has 30 minutes (counted while the store is open) to accept or decline. If the seller declines or does not respond in time, the order is cancelled and refunded in full.
  3. Seller prepares the food and marks it readySariKo then books a Lalamove driver automatically. If no driver can be booked after several attempts, the order is cancelled and refunded in full.
  4. Driver picks up and deliversThe buyer and seller follow the delivery status, driver details and live tracking link in the SariKo app.
  5. Order completedWhen Lalamove reports the delivery as completed, the order is marked as done automatically. The buyer should check the food on receipt; for any problem, see Section 06.
Section 06

Cancellations, Late or Failed Deliveries, and Problems with an Order

  • Before the seller accepts: the buyer may cancel free of charge and receives a full refund. Orders with a scheduled delivery time can be cancelled only until 24 hours before that time.
  • Seller running late: if the order is not ready within 1 hour after the seller accepts it (for scheduled orders, 1 hour after the delivery time), the buyer may cancel from the order page and receives a full refund. The order page shows this time.
  • After a driver is booked: the order cannot be cancelled in the app. Contact SariKo support if there is a problem.
  • Failed delivery: if Lalamove cannot complete the delivery, the seller may book a new driver or deliver the order personally. If the order is still undelivered 30 minutes later, the buyer may cancel and receives a full refund.
  • Missing, wrong, damaged or not-as-described food: contact support@sariko.store within 24 hours of receiving the order, with the order code, a description and photos. SariKo acknowledges the request within 24 hours and resolves it within 7 working days with a partial or full refund. Cooked food is not exchanged and does not need to be returned, unless SariKo needs it for a food-safety check.
  • Change of mind: no refund once the seller has accepted the order, except in the cases above.

Full refunds cover the whole amount paid, including the delivery fee, and are returned to the original payment method via VNPAY within 7 working days of approval.

Delays: SariKo is not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather or road closures beyond its control. For a significant delay, contact support@sariko.store.

Section 07

Seller Responsibilities

Sellers on SariKo are responsible for:

  • Accepting or declining paid orders within 30 minutes while the store is open
  • Preparing orders accurately and marking them ready within 1 hour of accepting (or by the scheduled delivery time)
  • Packaging food securely for motorcycle delivery to prevent spillage or damage
  • Keeping their store address and opening hours accurate, since drivers collect from that address
  • Handing the order to the driver, and rebooking a driver or delivering personally if a delivery fails
  • Cancelling promptly if they cannot fulfil an order, so the buyer is refunded

Sellers who repeatedly fail to fulfil orders or cause delivery failures may have their seller accounts reviewed or suspended.

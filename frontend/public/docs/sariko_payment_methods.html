Payment Methods

Version 2.1 Effective Date October 18, 2026 Operator Zen Tech Asia Co., Ltd.
This page discloses all accepted payment methods on the SariKo platform at www.sariko.store, as required under the Law on E-Commerce No. 122/2025/QH15 and Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP. All payments on SariKo are processed by VNPay, a payment intermediary licensed by the State Bank of Vietnam. SariKo is operated by Zen Tech Asia Co., Ltd. (MST 0316852554).
Section 01

Accepted Payment Methods — Buyers

All buyer payments on SariKo are processed securely through VNPay, a licensed payment intermediary regulated by the State Bank of Vietnam. The following payment methods are accepted:

Online Payment Available Now
VNPay QR

Scan the VNPay QR code at checkout with any Vietnamese bank's mobile banking app, or with the VNPay app. The most common payment method in Vietnam.

Domestic Card Available Now
Napas Debit Card

Vietnamese domestic debit cards on the Napas network are accepted. Issued by all major Vietnamese banks including Techcombank, Vietcombank, BIDV, and others.

International Card Available Now
Visa / Mastercard

International Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards are accepted. Foreign-issued cards are processed in VND at checkout. Your bank's own exchange rate applies.

E-Wallet Phase 2
MoMo / ZaloPay

Vietnamese e-wallet payments via MoMo and ZaloPay will be available in a future platform update. Not yet available at launch.

Platform fee for buyers: Zero. SariKo charges no fee on top of your order total. You pay the seller's listed price in VND plus the delivery fee. No hidden charges.
Section 02

How Payment Works

Buyers pay SariKo (Zen Tech Asia Co., Ltd.) through VNPAY when placing an order. Sellers are paid only for completed orders, on the payout schedule in Section 05.

1
Buyer pays at checkout via VNPAY

The full order amount (food + delivery fee) is paid through VNPAY. The payment link is valid for 15 minutes. The order is sent to the seller only after VNPAY confirms the payment.

2
Seller accepts and prepares the order

When the seller marks the order as ready, SariKo books a Lalamove driver automatically.

3
Order delivered

When Lalamove reports the delivery as completed, the order is marked as done automatically.

4
Seller receives the payout

Within 3 days after the order is completed, SariKo pays the seller the food amount, minus the service fee, VAT on that fee, and tax withheld on the seller's behalf under Decree 252/2026/NĐ-CP and Decree 68/2026/NĐ-CP. The delivery fee goes to Lalamove. See the fee schedule.

Cancelled orders are refunded to the buyer, not paid to the seller — see Section 06.
Section 03

Currency

Currency Policy
All prices on SariKo are displayed and charged in Vietnamese Dong (VND). This is the sole transactional currency on the platform in compliance with Vietnamese foreign exchange regulations.

If you pay with a foreign-issued Visa or Mastercard, your card issuer applies its own USD/VND (or other currency) conversion rate. SariKo has no control over and does not benefit from this exchange rate. We recommend checking your bank's rates before paying with a foreign card.
Section 04

Payment Security

All payments on SariKo are processed by VNPay, licensed by the State Bank of Vietnam as an authorised payment intermediary. VNPay processes payments within its own secure environment.

SariKo stores your bank account credentials Never Banking credentials are processed by VNPay only
Payment data encrypted in transit Always All connections to sariko.store use HTTPS/TLS encryption
Sellers paid only for completed orders Always Cancelled orders are refunded to the buyer instead

Section 05

Seller Payouts

Payouts are made within 3 days after an order is completed, by bank transfer in VND to a Vietnamese bank account in the seller's own name. No transfer fee is charged to the seller. Each payout comes with a statement listing every order, the fees and taxes deducted, and the amount paid.

The payout for an order under a buyer complaint is held until the complaint is resolved. If a buyer is refunded because of the seller's fault after the order has been paid out, that amount is deducted from the seller's later payouts, up to a percentage of each payout set by SariKo; any remainder is deducted from the following payouts.

Sellers register their bank account with the SariKo Operations team during onboarding. To change it, contact support@sariko.store.

Section 06

Refunds

Refunds are returned to the original payment method via VNPAY within 7 working days of approval. A full refund covers the whole amount paid, including the delivery fee.

  • Full refund, automatically: the buyer cancels before the seller accepts; the seller declines or does not accept in time; no driver can be booked; or the buyer cancels because the seller is late or the delivery failed.
  • Missing, wrong, damaged or not-as-described food: contact support@sariko.store within 24 hours of receiving the order. SariKo resolves the request within 7 working days with a partial or full refund.
  • Change of mind: no refund once the seller has accepted the order.

Details and time limits are in the Shipping & Delivery Policy, Section 06.

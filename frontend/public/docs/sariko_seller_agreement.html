  • The service fee is charged on the food amount of completed orders. It is not charged on delivery fees, nor on cancelled or refunded orders.
  • Fees exclude VAT, which is added at the rate in force when the fee is charged. SariKo pays the VNPAY payment gateway fees.
  • Founding Seller plan: a separate plan for the first 50 sellers. No monthly fee for the first 4 months, then 750,000 VND per month. Its 17% service fee stays fixed, unchanged, for as long as the Seller uses this plan.

  • Payouts are made every Tuesday for orders completed Monday to Sunday of the previous week (on the next working day if that Tuesday is a public holiday), by bank transfer in VND to a Vietnamese bank account in the Seller's own name, with a statement listing every order, the fees and taxes deducted and the amount paid. Each payout is the food amount of completed orders, less the service fee, VAT on the service fee and the tax withheld under section 4. The monthly fee is deducted from the first payout of each month.
  • Holds and deductions: the payout for an order under a buyer complaint is held until the complaint is resolved. If a buyer is refunded because of the Seller's fault after the order has been paid out, that amount is deducted from the Seller's later payouts. Each payout is reduced by no more than a percentage, set by SariKo, of the amount the Seller receives in that payout; any remainder is deducted from the following payouts.

Full fee schedule: sariko_fees.html.

3. Seller obligations

  • Sell only food the Seller cooks, which is lawful to sell.
  • Keep descriptions, photos and prices (in VND) accurate.
  • Hold a valid food safety certificate before starting to sell.
  • Accept or decline each order within 30 minutes while the store is open, and have accepted orders ready within 1 hour.
  • Pack food so it travels safely by motorbike.
  • Keep the pickup address and opening hours up to date.
  • Not move buyers off the platform to take orders or payments outside SariKo.
  • Hold one account per person.
  • Tell SariKo within 7 days when any information in the application changes.

4. Taxes

Under Decree 252/2026/NĐ-CP (Articles 43 to 46) and Decree 68/2026/NĐ-CP, SariKo withholds VAT and personal income tax on the food amount of each completed order and pays them to the tax authority on the Seller's behalf. The rates are set by the VAT Law (48/2024/QH15) and the Personal Income Tax Law (109/2025/QH15) and depend on whether the Seller is resident in Vietnam for tax purposes. Every home-cooked food Seller is in one business group, services attached to goods:

Business groupTax residentNon-resident or residency unknown
Home-cooked food (services attached to goods)4.5%5%
  • All home-cooked food sellers are in this group.
  • If the Seller's revenue for the year is 500 million VND or less, the Seller owes no VAT or personal income tax and can claim the tax withheld back from the tax office.
  • Tax withheld on an order that is later cancelled or refunded is returned to the Seller.
  • SariKo issues a certificate of tax withheld every year.
  • Foreign Sellers need only their passport for this; no Vietnamese tax code is required to start.
  • Any tax obligations in the Philippines (BIR) or another country remain the Seller's own responsibility.

5. Personal data

SariKo collects the Seller's identity documents, contact details, bank account and transaction data to run the seller account, make payouts, file taxes and meet its reporting duties under Vietnamese law. SariKo stores this data securely and only staff assigned to these tasks can see it. SariKo processes personal data in line with Vietnamese law on personal data protection; see the privacy policy.

6. Complaints and disputes

  • SariKo receives buyer complaints within 24 hours and resolves them within 7 working days.
  • When a refund is due to the Seller's fault, the payout for the order is held or the refunded amount is deducted from later payouts, as set out in section 2.
  • Disputes between SariKo and the Seller are settled first by negotiation, then by mediation through the platform's complaint process. If that fails, either party may refer the dispute to the competent state authority or the competent court in Ho Chi Minh City.
  • This agreement is governed by the laws of Vietnam.

7. Suspension and termination

  • The Seller may close the account at any time.
  • SariKo gives the Seller at least 5 days' notice, with reasons, before suspending, terminating or restricting the account, unless a state authority requires otherwise.
  • Money for completed orders is always paid out.
  • Information about dishes the Seller has listed is kept for at least 1 year (Decree 248).
